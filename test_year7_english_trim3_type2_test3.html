Detailed Correction

I- Reading Comprehension (5 marks)

1- True or False: (2 marks)

a) False. The text says "My friends and I cleaned up the park" and "Many voluntary people came to the park." They were volunteers; they did not get money. (The text does not mention any payment.)

b) True. The text states: "It was dirty. People threw litter everywhere." (paragraph 1) and "This is my park now. It's beautiful, clean and tidy." (paragraph 9).

2- Last autumn. (Text: "Last autumn, my friends and I cleaned up the park.")

3- Garbage = litter (Text: "there was a lot of litter" / "People threw litter everywhere." In paragraph 6, "collected all the garbage" confirms the synonymy.)

4- "They" (line 6) refers to many voluntary people (or the volunteers). (Text: "Many voluntary people came to the park... They were very helpful.")

II- Language (10 marks)

Exercise 1: Fill in the blanks (3 marks)

(1) Early – goes to school early.
(2) sharpener – a pencil, sharpener and a ruler.
(3) eraser – has also got an eraser.
(4) bell – the bell rings.
(5) raises – raises his hand.
(6) at – excellent at all subjects.
Extra words: headmaster, on.

Exercise 2: Circle the correct option (3 marks)

1. active (adjective describing Mrs. Johnson).
2. beginning – at the beginning of the lesson.
3. calls – calls the register (does the roll call).
4. date – write the date on the board.
5. clearly – explains the lessons clearly.
6. interesting – lessons are always interesting (adjective describing lessons).

Exercise 3: Put verbs in the correct tense/form (2 marks)

(get up) → gets up (present simple, 3rd person singular – "Every morning").
(go) → goes (present simple – habitual action).
(to have) → had (past simple – "Last week").
(dance) → dancing (gerund after "enjoyed").

Exercise 4: Match sentences with functions (2 marks)

1 → e) Offering help ("Can I help you?" is an offer of help).
2 → c) Expressing likes ("I want to buy..." expresses a desire/wish).
3 → d) Asking permission ("Can I see that one...?" asks for permission).
4 → b) Asking about price ("How much does it cost?" asks about the price).
Extra function: a) Taking leave (not matched).

III- Writing - Planning & sample (5 marks)

Planning (descriptive letter structure)

  • Greeting and opening: Address your penfriend, explain you're writing to describe your school.
  • Description of the school: Location, size, buildings, facilities (library, playground, labs), number of students/teachers, atmosphere.
  • Favourite subject(s): Name the subject(s), explain why you like them, what you learn, how the teacher makes it interesting.
  • Favourite teacher(s): Describe personality, teaching style, why you appreciate them.
  • Closing: Ask about your penfriend's school, express anticipation of reply, sign off.

Sample letter

Dear Emily,

Hi, how are you? In this letter I am going to describe my school and tell you about my favourite subjects and teachers. I hope you enjoy reading about it!

My school is called Zaouiet Jdidi Prep. School. It is a medium-sized school located in a quiet area surrounded by olive trees. The building is painted white and blue, and there is a large courtyard where we spend our breaks. We have a nice library, a computer lab, and a sports field where we play football and basketball. There are about 400 students and 25 teachers. The atmosphere is friendly and everyone knows each other.

My favourite subject is English. I love learning new words and expressions in English because I dream of travelling to England one day. We read interesting stories, do dialogues, and sometimes watch short videos in class. It is very exciting! My second favourite subject is Science because I am curious about nature and how things work. We do experiments in the lab, which is always fun.

My favourite teacher is Mrs. Johnson, our English teacher. She is kind, patient, and always smiles. She explains lessons clearly and encourages us to speak English without fear. When we make mistakes, she corrects us gently and says, "That's how we learn!" She also tells us funny stories about her trips to London. Thanks to her, I feel more confident in English.

What about you? Please write me back and tell me about your school and your favourite subjects. I can't wait to hear about your life in England!

Best wishes,
Your friend,
Amina
