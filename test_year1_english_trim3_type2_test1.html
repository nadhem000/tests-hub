THE TEXT

Home Is More Than A Building

(1) A few months ago, Pedro Ramirez, 45, lost his job in a grocery store. To pay the bills, he got a part-time job at night. Several days later, Pedro's wife, Luisa, gave him a big surprise. She was pregnant with their sixth child. Pedro was happy but worried. "How am I going to support another child without a full-time job?" he wondered.

(2) That evening, Pedro and Luisa got some more news. A fire was coming near their home. By the next morning, the fire was very close. The police ordered every family in the neighborhood to evacuate. The Ramirez family moved quickly. While Pedro was gathering their legal documents, Luisa grabbed the family photographs, and the children put their pets – a cat and a bird – in the family's truck. Then, the family drove to the home of their eldest daughter, one hour away.

(3) About 24 hours later, Pedro and Luisa got very bad news. The fire destroyed their home. They lost almost everything. With no home, only part-time work, and a baby coming, Pedro was even more worried about the future.

(4) For the next three months, the Ramirez family stayed with their daughter while workers were rebuilding their home. Many generous people helped them during that difficult time. Friends took them shopping for clothes. Strangers left gifts at their door. A group of children collected $500 to buy bicycles for the Ramirez children.

(5) Because of all the help from friends and neighbors, the Ramirez family was able to rebuild their lives. Two months after the fire, Luisa mailed out holiday cards with this message: "Home is more than a building. Home is wherever there is love."

III- Writing (15 marks)

a) Guided writing – Correct order (5 marks):

Complete paragraph: "I want to talk to my parents, but I can't. They're always too busy. There's always something more important than talking to me."


b) Free writing – Sample paragraph (10 marks):

When my English friend visits me in Tunisia, I prefer to speak to him in English. Although he can also speak Arabic, I feel that using English gives us both a chance to practise and improve our language skills. English is an international language, and the more we use it, the more confident we become. Moreover, speaking in English allows us to share cultural expressions that might not translate well into Arabic. It also helps my friend feel more comfortable since English is his native language. Sometimes, I use a few Arabic words to teach him something new about Tunisian culture, but most of our conversation is in English. In my opinion, mixing both languages can be fun, but English remains our main means of communication because it connects us and helps us grow together as global citizens.


Marking criteria – Guided writing: Correct order of sentences (2.5 marks) – Logical coherence (1.5 marks) – Punctuation & presentation (1 mark).

Marking criteria – Free writing: Relevance to topic (3 marks) – Clarity & organization of ideas (3 marks) – Grammar & vocabulary (2.5 marks) – Spelling & punctuation (1.5 marks).
