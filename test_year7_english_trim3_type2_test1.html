Detailed correction

I- Reading Comprehension (5 marks)

1- a/ False. People did not get money; they were volunteers who came to help clean up the park. The text says: "We put up posters and asked volunteers to help us" and "Many voluntary people came." (1 mark)

b/ True. The text states: "there was a lot of litter. It was dirty" (paragraph 1) and later: "This is my park now. It's beautiful, clean and tidy" (paragraph 9). (1 mark)

2- David and his friends cleaned the park last autumn. (Text: "Last autumn, my friends and I cleaned up the park.") (1 mark)

3- Garbage = litter (The word "litter" appears in paragraph 1: "there was a lot of litter" and is used synonymously with "garbage" in paragraph 6.) (1 mark)

4- "They" (line 6) refers to many voluntary people / the volunteers. (Text: "Many voluntary people came to the park... They were very helpful.") (1 mark)

II- Language (10 marks)

Exercise 1 (3 marks - 0.5 x 6):

1. Early (He always goes to school early.)

2. a sharpener (In his pencil-case: pens, a pencil, a sharpener and a ruler.)

3. eraser (He has also got an eraser.)

4. bell (At eight o'clock, the bell rings.)

5. raises (He raises his hand.)

6. at (He is excellent at all the subjects.)

Extra words (not used): headmaster, on

Exercise 2 (3 marks - 0.5 x 6):

1. active (She is very active.)

2. beginning (At the beginning of the lesson.)

3. calls (She calls the register.)

4. date (Write the date on the board.)

5. clearly (She explains the lessons clearly.)

6. interesting (All her lessons are always interesting.)

Exercise 3 (2 marks - 0.5 x 4):

1. gets up (Every morning she gets up at six o'clock - simple present, 3rd person singular.)

2. goes (She goes to the kitchen - simple present, routine.)

3. had (Last week, she had a party - simple past.)

4. dancing (They enjoyed dancing - gerund after "enjoy".)

Exercise 4 (2 marks - 0.5 x 4):

1 → e (Offering help) — "Can I help you?"

2 → c (Expressing likes) — "I want to buy a school bag for my son." (expressing a desire/like)

3 → d (Asking permission) — "Can I see that one over there, please?"

4 → b (Asking about price) — "How much does it cost?"

Extra function: a (Taking leave) — appears in "Good bye" at the end of the dialogue.

III- Writing (5 marks) - Sample letter

Planning (descriptive letter about school)

  • Greeting and opening: Greet penfriend, state purpose of the letter.
  • Description of school: Name, location, size, facilities (library, playground, labs).
  • Favourite subject(s): Name the subject, explain why you like it, what you learn.
  • Favourite teacher: Name, describe appearance/personality, teaching style, why you appreciate them.
  • Closing: Ask about their school, sign off warmly.

Sample letter

Dear Emily,

Hi, how are you? I hope you're doing well. In this letter I am going to describe my school and tell you about my favourite subject and teacher.

My school is called Zaouiet Jdidi Prep School. It is a beautiful school with white walls and a big garden full of flowers. We have a large library, a computer lab, and a nice playground where we spend our breaks. There are about 400 students and the atmosphere is always friendly.

My favourite subject is English. I love learning new words and expressions because it helps me communicate with people from other countries. I also enjoy reading stories and listening to songs in English. It's a very useful language and I dream of travelling one day.

My favourite teacher is Mrs. Soumaya. She teaches us English with a lot of energy and enthusiasm. She is kind, patient and always ready to help when we don't understand. Her lessons are never boring because she uses games, videos and songs. Thanks to her, I have made a lot of progress.

Please write me back and tell me about your school and your favourite subjects. I would love to know more about you.

Yours,
Amina
