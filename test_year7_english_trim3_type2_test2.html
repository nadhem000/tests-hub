Detailed correction

I- Reading Comprehension (5 marks)

1- ✓ b) took her class on a day out to the zoo. (The text clearly states Mrs Smith suggested a trip and explained they were going to the zoo.)

2- a) False. All the students were excited and cheered. The text says: "Everyone cheered and shouted with excitement." b) False. On the way back they talked about their favourite parts and were pleased. The text says: "they talked about their favourite parts of the day and were pleased."

3- The students felt amazed when they entered the zoo. (Text: "When they arrived at the zoo, the students were amazed.")

4- "them" (paragraph 2) refers to the animals. (Text: "They saw many animals. They even fed some of them.")

II- Language (9 marks)

Exercise 3 (3 marks - 0.5 each):

III- Writing (6 marks) - Planning and sample letter

Planning (descriptive letter structure)

  • Greeting and opening: Greet your friend, mention the recent school trip.
  • Where you went: Name the place (museum, park, zoo, historical site...).
  • What you saw: Describe the sights, exhibits, animals, nature, buildings.
  • What you did: Mention activities (picnic, guided tour, games, taking photos).
  • How you felt: Express emotions (excited, happy, amazed, a bit tired but glad).
  • Closing: Ask about your friend, suggest meeting soon, sign off.

Sample letter (approx. 13 lines)

Dear friend,

I hope you're doing well. I'm writing to tell you about our wonderful school trip last Thursday. We went to the natural history museum and it was fantastic!

We left school early in the morning by bus. When we arrived, a guide welcomed us and showed us around. We saw huge dinosaur skeletons, colourful butterflies, and sparkling minerals. My favourite part was the room full of ancient Egyptian mummies – it was both scary and fascinating!

After the visit, we had a picnic lunch in the museum garden. The weather was sunny and warm. We played games, took many photos and laughed a lot. Our teacher, Mrs Smith, was very kind and told us funny stories.

I felt so happy and excited all day long. It was the best school trip ever! I wish you could have been there with us.

Write back soon and tell me your news.

Yours,
[Your name]
